English

Short version

The developer does not collect any data. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app measures stays on your iPhone and on your own PC, unless you turn on one of the optional online features described below.

What the app uses and why

  • Camera (TrueDepth front camera): measures the position of your head for head tracking. Images are processed on the iPhone only; they are not recorded and not sent anywhere.
  • Headphone motion (AirPods sensors): follows head turns when the camera cannot see your face and recognises nods. Processed on the iPhone only.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: recognise your voice commands on the iPhone. Audio is not recorded and not transmitted; only the final command text is sent to the program on your PC. Option, off by default: if you turn on “Apple server recognition” (the app asks you first), your voice audio is sent to Apple for recognition under Apple’s terms.
  • Local network / USB cable: the iPhone talks only to the program on your own PC, over your local network or a cable. This connection is paired by you and encrypted. Head position, command text and the PC sound stream travel only between your iPhone and your PC.

What is stored

Settings, profiles and diagnostic logs are stored only on your devices (the iPhone and your PC). You decide whether to share a log, for example with the “Share” button; nothing is shared automatically.

Optional AI instructor

  • Apple Intelligence: if you use it, it runs on your iPhone. Option, off by default: if you turn on “Allow Apple server model” (the app asks you first), your questions and the instructor screen may be processed by Apple Private Cloud Compute under Apple’s terms.
  • Claude (Anthropic): only if you add your own Anthropic API key in the PC program and confirm its use. Then your questions and flight data are sent by the PC program to Anthropic under your key and Anthropic’s terms. Without your key, nothing is sent to Anthropic.

Bug report

The PC program can send us a bug report — only when you press “Send” yourself. Before sending, you see the list of files and can save the report on your PC instead.

What the report contains: the program’s log for the last 10 minutes (3 or 1 minute if the report is too large), its settings, the version, game, aircraft and language, and your “what happened” text if you wrote one. From your speech, only recognised commands are sent by default (e.g. “gear down”); everything else the microphone heard is replaced with “[speech hidden]”. The full text is attached only if you tick “attach everything I said” yourself.

Removed before sending: the Claude key and any other keys, passwords, and your Windows user name in file paths. Voice recordings, video and the iPhone camera image are never part of the report.

Where it is kept: in a private channel of our Discord server, visible only to the developers. Discord is a third-party service; its servers may be located outside the EU. We have no server of our own.

How long it is kept: until your issue is resolved, plus 90 days, then deleted. To have it deleted sooner, send us the report number (HTVI-…) on Discord or at liaison.studio.contact@gmail.com.

Third parties, children, changes

No data is sold or shared with third parties except Apple and Anthropic as described above, only at your choice, and Discord for a bug report you send yourself (see “Bug report”). The app is not directed at children under 13. If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
